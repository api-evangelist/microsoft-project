The new People view is now available for all Project users with a Project Plan 1 license and above! Whether you are a people manager, project manager, or another kind of manager, use this view in your projects to: Easily see each team member’s workload within your project Determine if the workload of each team member is over or under relative to one another Easily drag and drop tasks to reassign and update your team's tasks to balance out the work To give this view a try, simply open a project and navigate to the new "People" tab in one of your projects.
