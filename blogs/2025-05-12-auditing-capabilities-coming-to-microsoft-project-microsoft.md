Microsoft 365 auditing capabilities can help you track the types of operations performed within and by your Microsoft 365 services and give you a better picture of the most common activities across your environment. Users in your organization can use the audit log search tool to search for, view, and export (to a CSV file) the audit records for these operations. Thousands of user and admin operations performed in dozens of Microsoft 365 services and solutions are captured, recorded, and retained in your organization's unified audit log.
