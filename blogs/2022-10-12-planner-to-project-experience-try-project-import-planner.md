The feedback from many of you indicates that your users are acquiring software that is often outside of the “officially supported” IT infrastructure you are maintaining – and it’s causing you headaches! In response, Microsoft is building a solution to give you a managed way for your users to discover and try new apps and features that quickly and easily meet their needs within the controlled and secure environment you provide. Currently, Microsoft 365 users can use Microsoft Planner as a lightweight project management tool that enables better team productivity.
